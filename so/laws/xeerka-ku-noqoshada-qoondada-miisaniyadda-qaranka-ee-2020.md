## Qodobka 6

Haddii hayǯ ad loo sameeyo hormaris lacageed sababo la xidhiidha kharash maamul, horumariska waxaa loo diiwaangalinayaa sida hanti la bixiyey, ka dibna waa in la kharash gareeyo. Lama fasixi karo hormaris kale ayadoo la kharash gareyn kii hore.
